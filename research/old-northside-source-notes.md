## Channel-sync architecture findings

Airbnb’s official host guidance describes channel managers as tools that centralize availability and pricing, and directs hosts to authorize a supported channel manager and map their existing listings. Booking.com’s official Connectivity documentation states that its APIs are for Connectivity Partners that meet onboarding requirements, manage availability, reservations, and rates, and use provider-managed machine credentials. Expedia Group’s lodging documentation identifies separate Availability and Rates, reservation-management, and Product APIs for connectivity providers; its public overview documents webhooks for messaging and review events, but the reviewed overview does not establish a general reservation-webhook path for an independent inn website.

The practical implication is that a direct-booking website can be the inn’s source of truth, but production-grade two-way OTA synchronization should be implemented through a channel-management provider that the inn authorizes, unless the inn independently qualifies for each OTA’s connectivity program. The direct-booking application must therefore have a channel-adapter layer with idempotent booking records, room/rate mappings, event logs, and availability holds; it must not claim live Airbnb, Booking.com, or Expedia synchronization until the selected provider credentials, property identifiers, and channel mappings are connected and verified.
