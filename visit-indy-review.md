# Visit Indy Destination Review

The owner-approved destination, `https://www.visitindy.com/`, is reachable and presents Indianapolis tourism, dining, events, attractions, neighborhoods, and trip-planning content suitable for guests of Old Northside Bed and Breakfast.

The homepage Indianapolis section now displays an `Explore Visit Indy` action with an external-link icon. At desktop width, the call to action remains visible in the Indianapolis guide band and visually consistent with the site's existing gold-and-forest color system. The action is configured to open in a new tab with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`.
