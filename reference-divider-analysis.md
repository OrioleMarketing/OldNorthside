# Supplied Divider Reference Analysis

## Crops 1–2

The reference places a light gray, rounded contact bar across the hero-to-content boundary. The bar is visibly elevated above the transition rather than forming a full-width band. It uses teal pictograms, dark sans-serif labels, thin vertical separators, and a soft drop shadow. Below it, a dark diagonal band separates the image hero from the content panel; the content panel begins with a dark indigo/navy field and a warm gold headline. The requested implementation should borrow the hierarchy and overlap treatment, not the original website’s colors or exact styling.

## Crops 3–4

The bar’s middle repeats the same content pattern: compact icon, concise location or contact text, then a vertical separator. Its generous horizontal padding and large rounded corners make it read as a hospitality information card rather than a navigation ribbon. It crosses the diagonal boundary while leaving the hero image visible above and the content field visible below.

## Crops 5–6

The final segment introduces a visually distinct pill-shaped Book Direct action inside the contact bar. The bar terminates with a large rounded cap and shadow, while the lower content still begins behind a dark angled boundary. For Old Northside, the appropriate adaptation is a concise, neutral-paper floating contact card with a gold or forest direct-booking action. It should use the existing site’s icon, typography, and color system, omit the reference’s dark navy content background, and retain a subtle angled separator to establish a clear but lighter section boundary.
