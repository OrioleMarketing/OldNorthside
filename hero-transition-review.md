# Hero-to-House Transition Review

## Desktop review

The homepage now carries the hero image directly into a low-profile forest-green angled divider and a centered overlapping contact card. The card maintains a readable four-part hierarchy for city, street address, phone number, and direct booking. The house content beneath it uses the lighter accommodations paper palette, with dark body copy and gold heritage details providing clear contrast.

## Mobile review

At 390 px wide, the contact card stacks into compact, readable rows and preserves a full-width Book Direct action. The card overlaps the hero and lighter story section without covering the opening house label or heading. The dark angled divider is intentionally reduced at this width so it remains a subtle boundary rather than a visual band.

## Outcome

The refinement is visually coherent across the reviewed widths. The original hero copy now identifies the Dewenter-Greenen House as the current Old Northside Bed and Breakfast.
