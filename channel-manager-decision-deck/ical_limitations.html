iCal bridge: operating limit

iCal protects dates—
but not the full
booking journey

Calendar feeds are useful for blocking availability. They are not a real-time reservation, rate, payment, or restriction exchange.

Operating conclusionUse iCal as temporary availability protection—not as the long-term control plane.
30
minute sync cycleVrbo documents up to 20 minutes for imported events to appear, with calendar synchronization every 30 minutes.

iCal can protect

  • Blocked datesReservation and owner-held dates.
  • Basic availabilityA signal that a room should not be sold.
  • Low-complexity bridgingA practical interim link while mapping a full system.

iCal does not carry

  • Live commercial controlsRates, restrictions, and minimum stays.
  • Booking operationsPayment status, guest data, or confirmed-booking acknowledgement.
  • Safe circular workflowsTentative booking export/re-import can create payment issues.
