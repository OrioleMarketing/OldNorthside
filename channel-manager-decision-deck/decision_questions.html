Vendor meeting agenda

The next decision meeting should answer five questions

05

Do not leave a sales call with generic assurances. Leave with a mapped operating model, named owners, and written commercial terms.

Use the same questions with both eviivo and Beds24. The answers should be specific to Old Northside—not an example property.

01
Can the existing Expedia listing and all seven rooms be mapped without recreating the listing? Ask to see the room and rate-plan mapping path.
02
Will the connection synchronize availability, rates, restrictions, and reservations—not only iCal blocks? Ask for the exact Expedia capability scope.
03
How will the custom direct-booking site connect to the manager? Require the supported API, webhook, or iCal method and its limitations.
04
Who owns mapping, test bookings, cutover, and rollback support? Name the vendor and inn-side responsibilities.
05
What are the complete first-year costs and exit terms? Include subscription, onboarding, payment, support, cancellation, and any contract commitment.
Decision rule
A finalist is ready only when it can demonstrate the mapping and provide the commercial commitment in writing.
