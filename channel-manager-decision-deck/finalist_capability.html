Each offers a certified two-way connection, but they emphasize different operating experiences.

eviivo Suite

Preferred

eviivo positions its Expedia integration as an instant, two-way connection for independent accommodation operators.

  • Core flow: bookings, availability, and rates.
  • Broader scope: content, policies, promotions, fees, guest conversations, and reviews.
  • Operating fit: a more all-in-one hospitality workflow.

Beds24

Elite · 2025

Beds24 reports official two-way Expedia connectivity and its 2025 Expedia Elite Partner recognition.

  • Core flow: real-time booking import plus rates and inventory updates.
  • Control model: flexible room, rate, and channel configuration.
  • Operating fit: a more configurable, cost-conscious setup.
