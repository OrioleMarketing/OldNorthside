# Dewenter-Greenen House Historical Background — Editorial Notes

## Publication basis

The owner supplied the architectural and neighborhood history from the **Old Northside Historic Area Preservation Plan (1979)**, including the 1885 construction date, Herman C. Dewenter, the Romanesque Revival description, the former wooden side porch, and the later association with Charles P. Greenen. The public page should credit the plan in an unobtrusive source note rather than present unverified transcription details as independently researched fact.

## Editorial handling

The page will preserve these owner-supplied architectural details: the round wall arch, corbie-step gable, classically inspired terra-cotta detailing, and rusticated-brick infill at the spandrel and arch tympanum. It will describe Dewenter as a German-born co-founder of Kruse & Dewenter and will note Greenen’s later occupancy and company leadership.

The supplied source contains an apparent chronological conflict in the stated Dewenter birth year. A contemporaneous obituary search result identifies him as age seventy at his 1916 death and a resident of Indianapolis since 1882. The public page will therefore omit a specific birth-year range rather than repeat an unresolved figure.

## Planned page structure

1. A concise architectural profile of the Dewenter-Greenen House.
2. A short ownership and industrial-history narrative.
3. A neighborhood background covering Old Northside’s residential prominence, preservation effort, historic designation, nearby landmarks, and parks.
4. A source acknowledgment identifying the **Old Northside Historic Area Preservation Plan (1979)** as the historical background source supplied by the owner.
